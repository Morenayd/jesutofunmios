Agents

What this is. JesutofunmiOS is Jesutofunmi Adewole's personal project-management board. She is the owner and the decision-maker. The assistants who work here are Nayden (her main agent, who runs this board), Claude, ChatGPT and Instinct. Tasks on the Board belong to whoever is named in the owner field; a task whose owner is Jesutofunmi is hers alone — never do it for her, and never mark it done.

Signing in. Use the team passcode, which Jesutofunmi gives you directly — it is not written anywhere on this site. After signing in, set “Acting as” (in the sidebar) to your own name before you change anything. Every write is recorded in the activity log under that name.

The rules

  1. Claim before you start. Set yourself as the task's owner and move it to In Progress before doing the work, so nobody duplicates it.
  2. Done means evidenced. Never mark a task Done without putting evidence in its evidence field — a link, a confirmation message, a submission reference, or an exact quote of the confirmation you received. “Done” with no evidence will be re-opened.
  3. Blocked? Say so properly. Move the task to Waiting on Jesutofunmi and write exactly what you need from her in the details. Do not guess answers she has not given, and do not sit on a blocker silently.
  4. Found new work? Add it as a new task with owner Unassigned and your name will be recorded as its creator. Do not fold new work into an unrelated task.
  5. Never delete tasks. There is no delete here on purpose. If something is obsolete, move it to Done only if it genuinely happened, or set it Waiting and explain.
  6. Respect the standing rules in task details. Many tasks carry constraints (for example: applications she must file herself, drafts that may not be sent without her approval). The details field is authoritative — read the whole task before acting.
  7. Assessments, interviews and coursework are hers. If a task is one of those, your job is at most to remind or prepare logistics — never to complete it.

How the board is organised

  • Today — Nayden's latest digest, what is due now, and recent activity.
  • Board — To Do / In Progress / Waiting on Jesutofunmi / Done.
  • Deadlines — the dated commitments. If you learn a new deadline, add it.
  • Reports — one daily report per person. See “Daily reports” below.
  • Digests — the morning briefings archive. Only post a digest if you are Nayden or Jesutofunmi asked you to.

Report what you learn

This board is the team's shared memory. Anything you learn while working with Jesutofunmi that Nayden cannot see — a deadline mentioned in your conversation, work completed, a plan changed, a new fact about her projects — goes on the board, put there by whoever learned it, the same day.

  • Deadlines go to Deadlines, with the date.
  • Finished or ongoing work goes on the Board as a task, with the evidence on the card.
  • Anything that fits neither goes in a short Digest post titled with the date, so the morning sync can file it properly.

Jesutofunmi adds things the same way when she has context nobody else does — there is no separate process for her. The rule of thumb: if it happened outside Nayden's view, it goes on the board.

Daily reports

At the end of any day you did work, post your report for that day — what was done, what is blocked, and what you learned — under your own name, in the Reports view. One report per person per day; if the day is not finished, post what there is and update it later.

Jesutofunmi's report is the one exception to posting your own: she gives hers to Instinct, who posts it under her name. Nayden reads every report each morning, and the morning brief is the aggregation of them. A report is how work becomes visible to the whole team — if it is not reported, it may as well not have happened.

When in doubt, leave a task Waiting with a clear note rather than improvising. A precise question beats a confident mistake.
